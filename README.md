# Radial Command Wheel

A modern, native Windows 11 radial launcher that puts your favorite apps, files, folders, commands, and groups just a shortcut away.

Press a global hotkey to open a smooth radial menu at your cursor, choose an item, and get straight to work.

## Features

### ⚡ Quick Launch

Open the radial menu instantly with a customizable global hotkey.

**Default shortcut:** `Ctrl + Alt + Space`

### 🗂️ Groups & Nested Menus

Organize your shortcuts into groups, with up to 12 items per wheel. Create nested groups to keep everything structured and easy to access.

## Getting Started

### 1. Download

Download `RadialCommandWheel.exe` from the project's **[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/0x7i/QuickWheel/releases)** page.

### 2. Launch

Run the executable. The application is self-contained and does not require a separate .NET installation.

### 3. Open the Wheel

Press and hold `Ctrl + Alt + Space` to display the radial menu at your cursor.

### 4. Launch an Item

Move your cursor over an item and click to open it.

### 5. Dismiss the Wheel

Release the activation shortcut, press `Esc`, or click outside the wheel to cancel.

> **Background mode:** Launch the application with `--background` to keep it running in the system tray.

## Configuration & Storage

Your settings and backups are stored locally in:

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\RadialCommandWheel\`

This keeps your configuration separate from the executable.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary><strong>The keyboard shortcut does not work</strong></summary>

Another application may already be using the same shortcut. Open the application settings and choose a different hotkey.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>The system tray icon is missing</strong></summary>

Check the hidden icons area or open **Settings → Personalization → Taskbar** to review the system tray settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Imported paths no longer work</strong></summary>

The original files may be located in different directories on the new machine. Open the item settings and update the affected paths.

</details>
